# Issgard Atlas

Interactive map of every Episode 19 Issgard map for Ragnarok Online LATAM: portals, NPCs, quest spots,
monsters and drops, the daily quest board and a daily materials checklist, with a filter for the dailies
that give Ice Castle reputation. Names switch between English, Portuguese and Spanish.

**Open it: https://perazzoli-pedro.github.io/issgard-atlas/**

## Sources

- **LATAM client** (the game's own data files): map and item names, warps, NPC positions, monster spawns,
  quest text, the 04:00 reset, and the Ice Castle reputation name and cap.
- **[Divine Pride](https://www.divine-pride.net) (kRO data)**: top-down map renders, monster stats and drop rates.
- **[bROWiki](https://browiki.org) (bRO)**: quest rewards and reputation per daily.

The whole site is the single `index.html`. Your checklist ticks, language and filter choice are saved only
in your own browser.

Unofficial fan-made guide, not affiliated with Gravity or the Ragnarok Online LATAM server.
